# CR-01: Compact redesign (client change request, 1 Nov 2026)

## Summary
The client wants a **compact site**, so visitors reach what matters with less scrolling. The visual direction should follow the reference at `docs/reference/cr-01-home.png`. The client also wants a **tree-style team structure**.

This document overrides earlier specs wherever they conflict. After implementing it, update these files so they describe the new design:
- `docs/site-plan.md`
- `docs/design-system.md`
- `docs/motion-spec.md`
- `CLAUDE.md`

## 0. Ground rules (unchanged, and they win over the reference)
The reference is a **visual** source of truth: layout, colours, type style, shapes and proportions. It is **not** a content source. It was generated by an AI image tool, so parts of it are invented.

| In the reference | What we do | Why |

Log every string in `docs/client-requests.md` as "CR-01 copy: confirm", and give it a one-line revert to the previous live-site copy.

## 1. Compactness targets (measurable)
**Research basis:** NN/g's eye-tracking found about **57% of viewing time is above the fold** and **74% within the first two screenfuls**. So the hero, the three cards and About must sit in the first two screens.

| Page | Target |
|---|---|
| Home, desktop 1440×900 | total height ≤ **2.7 viewports** (about 2430px) excluding the footer. Hero + cards visible within 1.3 viewports. |
| Home, mobile 390×844 | ≤ **6 viewports** excluding the footer. The hero headline and the CTA visible without scrolling. |
| Inner pages | page hero ≤ 420px tall on desktop and ≤ 60vh on mobile. Section padding reduced site-wide (below). |

**Global spacing tokens:**
- Section padding: `py-12 md:py-16 lg:py-20` (was `py-20 md:py-28 lg:py-32`).
- Card padding: reduced by about 25%.
- Photo heights: capped so no single image exceeds about 70% of the viewport height.

**Remove all scroll-length-adding motion:** no pinned sections and no horizontal-scroll pins anywhere (this covers the Loans pin and the Process pin). A pinned section makes the page about 250vh longer.

Add a Playwright check that measures the document height for these targets on `/en/` and `/hi/`, at 1440 and 390.

**Contrast:**
- `#D71978` on white is about 4.9:1, which passes AA. Pink text on `blush` is lower (about 4.2:1), so any pink text smaller than 24px on blush uses `rani-600` or `ink`.
- Yellow is a fill only, with dark text on it.
- Verify everything with axe and the contrast script.

The plum utility bar and plum footer become magenta. Plum stays available only as a text colour.

**Typography.** Matches the reference and keeps Hindi working:

| Role | English | Hindi |
|---|---|---|
| Display / h1–h2 | **Merriweather** 700/900 | Tiro Devanagari Hindi |
| Body, nav, buttons | **Poppins** 400/500/600/700 (Google Fonts covers both Latin and Devanagari) | Poppins (Devanagari subset) |
| Script accents | **Dancing Script** 600/700 | Kalam (Dancing Script has no Devanagari) |

- This replaces Fraunces, Mukta and Kalam-for-English.
- Load only the weights listed. Keep the preload rules from Phase 3 (no Hindi font preloads on English pages).
- Re-measure LCP afterwards.

**Shapes:**
- The arch mask is retired on home.
- New signature frame: a **photo panel with a curved yellow border**. A rounded rectangle whose leading edge is an organic curve, with a 10–14px `marigold-400` curved band hugging it, as in the reference hero and About images.
- The yellow band is an SVG path, so it can **draw in** (DrawSVG) as the image reveals. This replaces the page-long Golden Thread on home.
- Cards: white, `rounded-2xl`, soft pink shadow, a circular icon disc (pink- or yellow-tinted), and a small round arrow button at the bottom.
- Decorative lotus or leaf outlines at about 10–15% opacity in section corners, drawn as SVG in code. Use them sparingly.

## 5. Team: leadership tree
The page sits at `/about/team/`. Build it as a **tiered tree**, grouped by the published titles:

```
                      [ Vikram Kumar Sinha ]
                            Chairman
                               │
                       [ Saurav Prakash ]
                     MD, CEO & Founder
                               │
               ┌───────────────┴───────────────┐
      [ Ranjeet Kumar ]                 [ Sumit Kumar ]
  Executive Director Business     Executive Director Finance
               └───────────────┬───────────────┘
          ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────┐
  [ Satyam Pushkar ]     [ Sonu Kumar ]      [ Shishir Kumar ]
  Head Business Dev.        Head IT      Chief Information Officer
```

- **Connectors run tier to tier** (a trunk plus a horizontal bar), **not person to person.** We don't know the real reporting lines, and drawing them would be inventing them.
  - Add "Please confirm who reports to whom" to the client list.
  - When the client confirms, the data model (`reportsTo` per member, optional) can switch to exact lines.
- **Tier labels** (proposed copy, log it): "Chairman", "Leadership", "Executive Directors", "Heads & Officers".
- **Cards:**
  - Circular **colour** photo, 112px on desktop and 88px on mobile, with a 3px yellow ring.
  - Name (Merriweather), role (Poppins), and the LinkedIn placeholder icon.
  - Hover or focus: lift and glow.
  - Chairman and MD/CEO cards are slightly larger.
- **Desktop:** centred top-down tree. Connector lines are SVG strokes in `marigold-400` that **draw in tier by tier** as the tree scrolls into view (DrawSVG, once); under reduced motion they're simply drawn.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** a **vertical tree**. A yellow spine runs down the left with tiers stacked; each tier's cards indent from the spine with a short branch line. No horizontal scrolling, no zoom or pan.
- **Accessibility:** mark it up as **nested lists** (`ul > li`) with tier headings, so the HTML itself is the text version of the chart (following the University of Missouri org-chart guidance). The connector SVG is `aria-hidden`.
- **Data:** in `src/data/team.ts`, add `tier` (1–4) and optional `reportsTo`. The order within tiers follows the crawled order.
- **Home:** no team section (compactness). About links to the Team page.
